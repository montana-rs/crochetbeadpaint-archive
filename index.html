CrochetBeadPaint (offline recovery build)

This is a community-recovered offline rebuild of the crochetbeadpaint.info bead-crochet pattern editor, reconstructed from its Wayback-Machine-archived client-side JavaScript (application-latest.js, captured 2022-10-07) after the original site went permanently offline. It runs entirely in your browser — patterns save to localStorage via the app's built-in offline mode (the original RaportLocalStore class). Server-only features (sharing, comments, community gallery, sound packs served from /images/sounds/, login) are not available. Export to PNG / PDF / DBB / JBB works locally.
